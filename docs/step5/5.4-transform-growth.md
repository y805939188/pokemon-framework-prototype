# 5.4 Transform growth repair

## Scope and contract

The real App → BattleSession → pinned Gen8 Showdown path can award experience while the
recipient is transformed. WP42 §4.2 requires level/current HP/max HP to follow the original
member's growth without replacing the five copied battle stats. WP47-B §6 likewise keeps
Transform's HP, level and permanent moves distinct from the copied target.

At baseline `44ca0c2d008f929b990f65917a8965a6efed69a0`, `BattleGateway.applyGrowth` used
`p.species.baseStats`, which is the transformed target, both for permanent stat verification
and the native HP recalculation. It also overwrote the copied battle stats. A synthetic,
authored Ditto legally transforming into Pidgey and then KOing it with copied Seismic Toss
reached this path through the production App and threw `stat mismatch for P1.hp`. Both
same-level EV/EXP gains and an actual level-up failed; the EXP-disabled control passed.
This establishes supported author-content reachability, not default encounter availability.

## Repair

- Only while transformed, calculate permanent stats from the retained `baseSpecies`.
- Always update permanent level, EVs and base stats. Retain the copied non-HP battle stats
  and cached speed while transformed; ordinary non-transformed growth remains unchanged.
- Invoke the pinned engine's own HP recalculation with the original species selected only
  for that synchronous call, restoring the battle species in `finally`. This retains the
  engine's HP-delta, fainted and notification behavior without a second HP formula.
- Keep the stat mismatch check, normal learned-move handling, checkpoint protocol and
  permanent result/commit contract in place. No Showdown source or core growth rule changes.

## Regression coverage and validation

`experiments/step5/4/test-transform-growth.cjs` uses compiled synthetic author content and
real production App/Session actions. Engine access only reads temporary stats. The initial four cases
cover same-level HP-EV threshold growth, level-up, nonterminal and terminal KOs,
checkpoint restore, copied PP versus permanent PP, switch-back to grown natural stats,
EXP-disabled behavior, abort without persistence, and exactly-once normal final commit.
The suite is registered in `scripts/test-headless.sh`.

## Independent-review revision: permanent public details

The first candidate `6eefb2129ff4a31d488372248a8eb2d8678a9df0` fixed the stat/HP failure,
but independent review found a P2 in the unchanged public-details refresh. After restoring
the copied battle species, `getUpdatedDetails()` wrote that target into permanent `details`.
Switch-back cleared Transform and restored Ditto's actual stats/species but still announced
Pidgey in the `switch` event and side request.

The revision refreshes public details within the same original-species selection used by
the native HP helper, before the `finally` restores the temporary battle species. Metadata
such as shiny/gender is preserved; the newly grown level is reflected in original-species
details. Copied battle species, stats, moves, types and ability remain temporary.

Coverage now has five cases. Both normal and checkpoint-restored switch-back paths check
the actual public `switch` event plus both Session and Gateway side requests. Permanent
details are checked during transformed growth and after checkpoint restoration too.
Against the first candidate's unchanged runtime, the revised suite produced four expected
wrong-public-species failures and one passing EXP-disabled control. After the revision,
eight files passed 81/81, zero fail/cancel/skip/todo. This count includes the five Transform
cases, five level-stat display cases and two pinned-engine contract cases. Engine build,
framework/core/app typechecks and import boundaries also passed again.

The first candidate, its logs and runtime are retained. These are author checks, not an
independent acceptance. Full quick/headless, plugins, SDK, CI and NW remain unexecuted.
