# 5.4 Trainer AI public type transitions

Candidate based on `47c6abec6bb92c95112b958f08c5db4c861b6184`; independent review pending.

The public collector previously handled only switch/drag/replace and explicit typechange/typeadd.
It reset an Illusion reveal as if it were a switch and missed Transform, Forecast formechange,
and Mimicry's typechange end. Legal authored teams could therefore receive incorrect AI damage
scores without an engine rules error.

## Repair

- Maintain separate original, current base, and added public types for both single-battle active slots.
- Illusion `replace` corrects the revealed species type without discarding public type changes/additions.
- `-transform` copies the target's public projection, never player-side engine internals.
- `-formechange` updates current types and clears the prior addition; permanent `detailschange`
  also updates original types. Mimicry `-end typechange` restores original types.
- Real switches reset the projection. Both projections are checkpointed and restored with their owner.
- No policy formula, persistent schema, player request visibility, or battle outcome changes.
