# Player battle names

The formal Scene now uses a saved player nickname, falling back to localized species when absent, consistently in battle messages, status cards and the party picker. Learning and management already used that policy. A normal named acquired member enters the next battle through the unchanged persistent-member/Session projection; no save schema or battle identity changes are needed.

Only player-owned names change. Opponent cards continue using the published appearance or Unknown, never a private nickname. This does not close the existing Illusion/public-appearance limitations, and eggs retain their non-disclosing label.

`test-battle-summary-scene.cjs` adds English/Chinese compiled Scene tests: named player action and switch messages, active status appearance before/after switching, stable-ID party rows, no persistent writes, opponent nickname non-consumption, and unnamed species fallback. Fixed 47c plugin fails both named tests; the fallback passes. The candidate passes all three. These tests use controlled VM window/input ports and the real App/Session/Gen8 engine, not NW or human input/hearing acceptance.

No NW, P4, CI or full 5.4 acceptance is implied.
